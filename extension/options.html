geneanet-drift options

The part of the tree's address before _w, for example exampletree from https://gw.geneanet.org/exampletree_w.

People edited after this date are listed first under To visit.

Saved pages

The extension keeps a list of the pages it has saved. Resetting clears that list so the pages are saved again when you open them. It does not delete any files.

Access to Geneanet

The browser has not yet allowed this extension to read gw.geneanet.org pages, so nothing is saved.
